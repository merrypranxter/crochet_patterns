# Yarn Materials

Yarn material controls fuzz, twist, sheen, fiber thickness, halo, stiffness, and drape. Cotton reads crisp. Wool reads fuzzy. Acrylic reads smooth and springy. Metallic yarn reads sharp and reflective.

## Visual Checklist

- Are individual loop paths visible?
- Are the stitch units readable?
- Is the yarn behaving like a fiber, not plastic cord?
- Do shadows belong to the stitch relief?
- Does the pattern show row, round, or motif logic?

## Prompt Vocabulary

hook-built loops, yarn twist, soft fiber fuzz, stitch relief, chain spaces, repeated motifs, looped texture, handmade tension variation, textile surface.
