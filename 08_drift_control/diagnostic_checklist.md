# Diagnostic Checklist

Ask these questions before accepting a crochet-style output:

- Can I identify individual stitch units?
- Does the surface show loops or chain spaces?
- Is there a row, round, motif, or grid logic?
- Does the yarn have thickness and fiber behavior?
- Is it clearly crochet rather than knitting?
- Are increases/decreases or repeats plausible?
- Does the colorwork follow a crochet-friendly structure?
- Are holes intentional instead of accidental gaps?
