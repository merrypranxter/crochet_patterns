# Hyperbolic Crochet

A mathematical crochet surface created by frequent increases.

## Core Construction Logic

This pattern family is defined by repeat units, stitch placement, growth direction, and the relationship between yarn-filled areas and open spaces.

## Visual Traits

- ruffled edges
- coral growth
- exponential perimeter

## Use Cases

- math art
- coral forms
- non-Euclidean textile surfaces

## Generative Translation

Represent the pattern as a grammar of stitch tokens. Treat repeats as loops, corners as rule changes, and color rounds as layer changes. For shader translation, convert stitch bodies into repeated soft glyphs with yarn-fiber perturbation and tiny shadow relief.

## Prompt Module

`hyperbolic crochet crochet pattern, visible hook-built loop structure, repeating stitch units, yarn texture, handmade tension variation, textile surface design, chart-readable construction logic`

## Anti-Drift

Keep the stitch logic visible. Do not let it collapse into generic lace, generic yarn, or printed ornament.
