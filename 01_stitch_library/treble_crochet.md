# Treble Crochet

A very tall stitch that creates elongated posts and open fabric.

## Visual Traits

- long columns
- large gaps
- lace-like height

## Construction Logic

A stitch can be modeled by its insertion target, yarn-over count, draw-through sequence, height, and resulting top loop. For generative systems, these become parameters rather than literal craft steps.

## Useful For

- lace
- doilies
- dramatic openwork

## Prompt Vocabulary

Use phrases like: `treble crochet`, hook-built loops, visible yarn path, stitch bodies, chain spaces, loop texture, tactile relief, handmade tension variation.

## Drift Warnings

Do not turn this into generic knitted texture. Crochet stitches have knots, posts, chain spaces, and hook-built loop paths.
