# Post Stitches

Stitches worked around the body of a previous stitch rather than into its top loop.

## Visual Traits

- raised cables
- shadowed relief
- woven-looking ribs

## Construction Logic

A stitch can be modeled by its insertion target, yarn-over count, draw-through sequence, height, and resulting top loop. For generative systems, these become parameters rather than literal craft steps.

## Useful For

- cables
- waffle texture
- basketweave effects

## Prompt Vocabulary

Use phrases like: `post stitches`, hook-built loops, visible yarn path, stitch bodies, chain spaces, loop texture, tactile relief, handmade tension variation.

## Drift Warnings

Do not turn this into generic knitted texture. Crochet stitches have knots, posts, chain spaces, and hook-built loop paths.
