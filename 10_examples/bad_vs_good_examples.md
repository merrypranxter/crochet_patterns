# Bad Vs Good Examples

## Bad

```text
cozy yarn pattern, soft knitted texture, pretty handmade blanket
```

Why it sucks: vague, knitting drift, no crochet structure.

## Good

```text
crochet ripple chevron blanket pattern, rows of double crochet stitches shaped by regular increases and decreases, visible hook-built loops, peaks and valleys, alternating acidic color stripes, yarn twist and stitch-local shadows
```

Why it works: names the pattern family, stitch type, shaping logic, visual texture, and color system.
